What may move prices?

Copper momentum

Usually the largest raw-material influence on brass. A rising copper benchmark can support brass pricing.

⇄
Zinc and alloy mix

Zinc affects brass replacement cost; the actual effect depends on grade and copper-to-zinc ratio.

₹
INR, demand and local premium

Currency, freight, inventory, local demand and supplier margins can outweigh a simple exchange-price signal.
